Privacy Policy

Learn how we collect, use, and protect your personal information on Canzi.

1. Introduction

Done is committed to protecting the privacy of its users, partners, and delivery agents. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal data when using our services via the application or website.

We understand the importance of privacy and data protection in the digital age and are committed to maintaining the highest standards to ensure the safety of your personal information. This document is the main reference for understanding your rights and our obligations.

By accessing this document, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to our data collection and processing practices described herein. We encourage you to review this policy regularly for updates.

2. Scope of Application

This policy applies to all users of the Done application, including customers, service providers (partners), and delivery agents. It governs data collection, processing, and sharing within the Done platform.

This policy does not apply to external websites or services linked to our platform. It has been prepared in accordance with Law No. 08-09 on the protection of individuals regarding personal data processing.

It applies to all devices (smartphones, tablets, computers) and all regions where the platform operates. In case of conflict with other agreements, this policy prevails unless otherwise stated.

3. Data We Collect

We collect different types of data to provide efficient and safe services.

3.1 Identity Data:
Full name
Email address
Phone number
Date of birth
Nationality

3.2 Location Data:
Real-time location during app usage
Pickup and delivery addresses
Saved locations

3.3 Usage Data:
Order and transaction history
Date and time of actions
Device type and operating system
IP address and browser type

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on lawful grounds, including user consent, contractual necessity, legal obligations, and legitimate interests such as improving our services.

You may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the legality of processing carried out before withdrawal.

5. Purpose of Data Use

We use your data only for necessary and defined purposes.

Core purposes:
Provide delivery services
Manage user accounts
Process payments
Communicate about orders

Secondary purposes:
Improve services
Analytics and reporting
Fraud detection
Legal compliance

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share data with trusted partners such as payment providers, delivery partners, cloud providers, and authorities when required by law.

In case of merger or acquisition, data may be transferred with prior notice and deletion options.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data while your account is active and as required after closure.

Transaction data: 5 years
Identity data: 3 years after contract end
Usage data: up to 1 year

After that, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

8. Data Security

We use encryption (SSL/TLS, AES-256), access control, and security audits to protect your data.

However, no system is fully secure, so users must protect their credentials.

9. Data Breach Management

We notify authorities within 72 hours in case of a breach.

Users will be informed if risk is high.

Response includes isolation, investigation, recovery, and security improvements.

Users will be notified 30 days before major updates.
